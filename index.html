Privacy Policy

Exam Checker: AI Paper Marking (package com.examchecker.app) · Developer: Mikao · Effective 8 October 2026

In short: Exam Checker helps teachers make exam papers, scan students' answer sheets and check them. Scans, student names, roll numbers and marks stay on your phone. When the app needs AI to suggest a mark or write a paper, it sends only the question, your answer key and the student's answer, never a name, roll number, class, school name or a full page. We have no accounts, and we don't sell data. Ads, where shown, come from Google AdMob.

Who this policy is for

Exam Checker is made for teachers and schools. It is not directed at children. Teachers enter information about their students (such as names, roll numbers, answers and marks) to do their work; the teacher or school decides what to enter and remains responsible for it under the rules that apply to them. This policy explains what the app does with that information.

What stays on your phone

  • Classes, student names and roll numbers, exams, questions, answer keys, scanned pages, the text read from them, AI suggestions, your marks and results are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Other apps can't read it, and Android's device encryption protects it.
  • Reading text and bubbles from scanned pages, and checking objective answers and answers that match your key, happens on the phone with no internet.
  • API keys you enter for AI providers are encrypted with the Android Keystore.
  • Scanned pages, downloaded AI models and API keys are excluded from Android cloud backups. Other app data (such as your classes and marks) may be included in your own Android device backup if you have backup turned on; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account, not by us.

The app's AI service only passes your request to the AI model and returns the answer; it does not store the content of your requests. Like any internet service, it briefly uses your device's IP address to limit how many requests one device can make. Google Gemini and other providers process requests under their own terms and privacy policies. You can turn off AI checking in Settings › AI & processing; the app then checks only what it can on the phone and leaves the rest for you to mark.

Advertising

The app may show ads from Google AdMob. When it does, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and information about ad interactions and app performance, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice in the app's settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Ads never receive scans, student information, questions or marks.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell personal data and don't share it with third parties for their own purposes. Information leaves the phone only as described above, or when you choose to share something yourself (for example exporting results as CSV or sharing a paper PDF through Android's share sheet).

Keeping and deleting data

  • Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete an exam with everything in it (menu › Delete exam), remove a single scanned page, delete scanned pages of finished exams in Settings › Data & privacy, or uninstall the app to remove everything.
  • Full page photos of finished exams are deleted automatically after the period you choose in Settings › Data & privacy (30 to 365 days). Cropped answers and marks stay so results can still be reviewed.
  • We don't keep copies of your scans, answers or marks on our servers, so there is nothing for us to delete; questions about data handled by AI providers or AdMob can be sent to them or to us.

Security

All connections the app makes use encryption (HTTPS). Data on the phone is kept in private app storage protected by Android, and API keys are encrypted with the Android Keystore.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
